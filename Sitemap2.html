RECORDED: SEPTEMBER 2023 // CURATED EXCERPTS & DIALOGUE
JANA LETONJA // ON EVOLVING AS AN ARTIST

Over nearly two decades on screen, you've transitioned from young-adult sensations to complicated, grounded roles. How has your relationship to acting shifted as you've deepened your own sense of self?

LUCY HALE // REFLECTION

When you start so young, you spend years operating on adrenaline, trying to be what everyone else envisions. As I grew up and made deliberate changes in my personal life—getting sober, learning to sit with silence—my work transformed. I stopped seeking validation through work and started seeking truth and emotional honesty.

JANA LETONJA // ON VULNERABILITY & GROUNDING

You've spoken candidly about mental health, self-acceptance, and creating sanctuary in your life. Does being that transparent feel daunting in this industry?

LUCY HALE // REFLECTION

It felt terrifying until the moment I did it. Vulnerability used to feel like giving away power; now I understand it is where genuine power begins. Surrounding myself with things that keep me steady—my dogs, spaces that bring quiet, friends who know me without the external noise—has made all the difference.

JANA LETONJA // ON FUTURE CHAPTERS

What guides your creative compass now as you look forward to directing, producing, and taking on new narratives?

LUCY HALE // REFLECTION

Curiosity rather than fear. I want to build things that resonate in real ways. Whether that is behind the camera or in the choices I make day to day, the goal is staying rooted in what feels authentic.
